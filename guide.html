How to claim your FABLES airdrop

FABLES went live on Robinhood Chain on October 20, 2026. Points claims and PROLOGUE redemption are open now through November 20. Here is exactly how eligibility works and how to claim in a few minutes.

The snapshot

Your allocation is based on the PROLOGUE snapshot taken at block 91,198,392 on October 17 at 20:09:36 UTC. The final redemption rate is fixed at about 37.68049 eligible PROLOGUE per FABLES. Moving PROLOGUE out of your wallet before you redeem voids the claim for that balance.

Eligibility

  • You held eligible PROLOGUE at the snapshot block, or earned points in the Fables program.
  • The selection window closed October 19 at 02:00 UTC — the claim option you saved (Liquid, Six-month lock, or Auto-max) is the one you receive.
  • Total claimable allocations are about 67.06M FABLES: 24.69M liquid, 20.90M in six-month locks, and 21.48M auto-max.

How to claim

  1. Add Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663) to your wallet and keep a little ETH on-chain for gas.
  2. Open the official claim portal and enter the wallet that earned your points.
  3. The portal reads your snapshot balance, applies eligibility checks, and shows your final entitlement.
  4. Pick your option (Liquid / Six-month lock / Auto-max) and sign the claim locally in your browser.
Note: Viewing your allocation is read-only — it never asks for your private key. Key material is only used at the final claim step, signed locally in your browser, then discarded.
