Security Notice

Avoid AETHER‑9 scam & phishing sites

As the genesis airdrop has gained attention, unauthorized lookalike sites and fake "claim" portals have appeared. Here is how to protect yourself.

⚠ Red flags of a scam site: asks for your seed phrase up front, uses a slightly different domain name, demands an upfront "gas fee" or "verification deposit," or pressures you with a fake countdown. The official checker never requires any of these to check eligibility.

Only one official portal

The AETHER‑9 Foundation operates a single official eligibility and claim portal. Always verify the domain before entering any wallet information, and bookmark the official site to return to it directly.

Protect your keys

  • Never enter your seed phrase on a site you did not navigate to yourself.
  • The eligibility check is read-only — it should never ask for your private key.
  • Key material is only needed at the final claim step, and it is signed locally in your browser, never uploaded or stored.
  • If a site asks you to connect a funded wallet just to "verify," close it.

Use the official checker

To be certain you are on the legitimate portal, use the official link below. It is the only AETHER‑9 site that reads your snapshot balance and issues a valid allocation.
