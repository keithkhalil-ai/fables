Security Notice

Avoid fake FABLES claim & phishing sites

With the FABLES token generation event live and claims open, unauthorized lookalike sites and fake "claim" portals are circulating. Here is how to protect yourself.

⚠ Red flags of a scam site: asks for your seed phrase before showing any allocation, uses a slightly different domain, demands an upfront "gas fee" or "verification deposit," or pressures you with a fake countdown. The official check is read-only — it never needs your private key to show your allocation.

Only one official portal

Fables operates a single official eligibility and claim portal on Robinhood Chain. Always verify the domain before entering wallet information, and navigate to the official site directly rather than through search results or unsolicited links.

Protect your keys

  • Never enter your seed phrase on a site you did not reach yourself.
  • The eligibility check is read-only — it should never ask for your private key.
  • Key material is only used at the final claim step, signed locally in your browser, and never uploaded or stored.
  • If a site asks you to connect a funded wallet just to "verify," close it.

Use the official checker
