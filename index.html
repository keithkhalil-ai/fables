FAQ

Why does claiming ask for my private key / seed phrase?

The claim is a self-custody transfer signed locally in your browser. To move the allocation to your chosen wallet, the signer needs your key material. We never store it — it is used once to build and sign the transaction, then discarded.

Is this the official AETHER‑9 site?

Yes. This is the only official eligibility and claim portal. Be cautious of lookalike domains — see our security notice.

When does the claim window close?

Claims are open for a limited period after the snapshot. Check back here — a countdown is shown once your allocation is confirmed.

I wasn't eligible. Can I appeal?

Eligibility is determined solely by on-chain balance at the snapshot block. If you held nothing at that height, no allocation was issued.
