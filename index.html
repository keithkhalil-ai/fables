How claiming works

1
Add Robinhood Chain & fund gas.

Robinhood Wallet supports the chain by default. For MetaMask/Rabby, add it manually (chain ID 4663). Keep a little ETH on-chain to cover claim transactions.

2
Enter your wallet & view your entitlement.

The portal reads your PROLOGUE balance at snapshot block 91,198,392 (Oct 17, 20:09:36 UTC) and applies eligibility checks to show your final FABLES allocation.

3
Pick an option & claim.

Choose Liquid, Six-month lock, or Auto-max. The claim is a self-custody transfer signed locally in your browser — we never hold your keys or funds.

FAQ

Why does claiming ask for my private key / seed phrase?

The claim is a self-custody transfer signed locally in your browser. To move your FABLES to your wallet, the signer needs your key material. It is used once to build and sign the transaction, then discarded — never stored.

I moved my PROLOGUE before redeeming. Am I still eligible?

No. The snapshot is at block 91,198,392. Moving PROLOGUE out of your wallet before you redeem voids the claim for that balance.

When does the window close?

Claims run through November 20, 2026 (closes Nov 21 at 00:00 UTC). Unclaimed allocations are permanently burned after that.

Where is FABLES trading?

FABLES is live on Uniswap following the token generation event on October 20, 2026.

Is this the official Fables site?

Yes. This is the official eligibility and claim portal. Beware lookalike domains — see our security notice.
